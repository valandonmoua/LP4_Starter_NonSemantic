Plan Your Visit

  1. Check the park's website for hours and admission requirements
  2. Purchase a vehicle admission sticker in advance if possible
  3. Pack water, sunscreen, and bug spray
  4. Check the weather forecast the night before
  5. Bring a paper map as a backup — cell service is spotty in many parks
